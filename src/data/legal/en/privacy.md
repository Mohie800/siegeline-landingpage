This policy explains what data the mobile game **SIEGELINE** (Android package
`com.siegeline.game`, "the game") collects, why it is collected, and what you can do
about it. The game is published by **Mohyeldeen** ("we", "us"), who is the controller
of that data.

Contact for anything in this document: **support@mohyeldeen.dev**.

## The short version

- The game creates an account for you automatically so your progress can be saved.
- We collect what is needed to run matches, save progress, and rank players — nothing else.
- **There are no ads, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.**
- We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for marketing.
- You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time: [request deletion](/account-deletion).

## What we collect

We do not ask you for your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth.

**Account data**

- A **device identifier** generated by the game and stored on your device. This is how
  your account is recognised when you reopen the game; it is not your advertising ID.
- Your **Google Play Games player ID**. The game signs you in to Play Games in the
  background, and our server exchanges a one-time code with Google for this ID and stores
  it against your account. It is what lets us give your account back to you if you
  reinstall the game or move to a new phone, because the device identifier above does not
  survive either. We ask Google for the games scope only — we never receive your Google
  email address, your name, or your profile.
- The **display name** you choose in game.
- Your **friends list**, friend requests, and challenges you send or receive.

**Gameplay data**

- Progression: unit and card levels, card copies, chests, gold and gems.
- Match records: results, trophies, rank, and recent match history.
- Leaderboard entries — your display name and trophy count are visible to other players
  on in-game leaderboards and to the players you are matched against.

**Technical data**

- Your **IP address** and connection metadata, processed by our game servers while you
  are connected so the match can reach you.
- Server-side logs of errors and of requests to our backend, used to diagnose problems
  and to detect abuse.

**What we do not collect:** contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera
data, advertising identifiers, or any special-category personal data.

## Why we use it

| Purpose | Data used |
| --- | --- |
| Create and restore your account | Device identifier, Play Games player ID, display name |
| Run matches and matchmaking | Account data, gameplay data, IP address |
| Save progression and inventory | Gameplay data |
| Leaderboards, ranks, and match history | Display name, trophies, match records |
| Friends, parties, and challenges | Display name, friends list |
| Detect cheating and abuse, and enforce our [Terms](/terms) | Account data, technical logs |
| Answer your support and deletion requests | Whatever you send us, plus account data |

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on **performance of a contract** (running
the game you asked to play) and on our **legitimate interests** in keeping the game
working, fair, and secure.

## Who else sees it

- **Other players** see your display name, trophies, rank, and leaderboard position.
- **Our hosting provider** operates the servers on which the game backend and its
  database run, and therefore stores the data on our behalf.
- **Google Play** distributes the app and handles the download itself. Google's own
  handling of that is covered by Google's privacy policy, not this one.
- **Google Play Games Services** signs you in and gives our server your Play Games player
  ID so that your account can be recovered on a reinstall or a new phone. We send Google
  only the one-time code needed to obtain that ID, and Google's own handling of your Play
  Games account is covered by Google's privacy policy, not this one.
- We will disclose data if we are legally required to.

That is the complete list. There are no advertising networks, data brokers, or analytics
vendors involved.

## Where it is stored, and for how long

Game data is stored in a database on servers we control, and is transmitted over
encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS). Access to the servers is restricted to the developer.

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. When you ask us to delete
it, we act on the request within **30 days**. We may keep a minimal record of a ban or
of confirmed abuse after deletion, and ordinary server logs may persist for a short
period in routine backups before rotating out.

## Your choices and rights

You can:

- **Delete your account and its data** — see [Account deletion](/account-deletion).
- **Ask what we hold about you**, and ask us to correct it.
- **Object to processing** or ask us to restrict it.
- **Complain to your local data protection authority** if you are in a region that
  provides one.

Email **support@mohyeldeen.dev** for any of these. Because accounts are not tied to an
email address, we may need to ask you for details that only the account holder would
know before we act on a request.

Uninstalling the game does **not** delete your account — the account stays on our
servers so your progress can be restored. Use the deletion page if you want it gone.

## Children

The game is not directed at children. It is intended for players aged **13 and over**
(or the minimum age required in your country, if that is higher). We do not knowingly
collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has created an
account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the top of the page changes with it,
and the current version always lives at this URL. Material changes will also be
announced in the game.
